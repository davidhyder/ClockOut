Clock Out on your iPhone

Install it once and it opens full screen from its own icon, like any other app.

Open Clock Out

Install (about a minute)

  1. Use Safari. Tap Open Clock Out above so the calculator itself is on screen.
  2. Open the Share menu:
    • iOS 26: tap ••• at the bottom right, then Share.
    • Older iOS: tap the Share button ⬆︎ (a square with an arrow) in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on.
  5. The name shows as Clock Out. Tap Add.

The clock icon is now on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.

Using it

  • Tap a time to open the keypad. Type the digits; the last two are the minutes (705 = 7:05, 105 = 1:05, 1130 = 11:30). Tap AM or PM to save and move to the next punch.
  • "Clock out at" shows when to leave to hit your target hours. Hours show as h:mm and as decimals (8:15 · 8.25).
  • Tap into a day to outline it; the top button then clears just that day. Clear all erases everything.

Good to know

  • Your punches are saved only on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Removing the icon from your home screen also erases its saved punches.
  • After the first open it works without a connection, and it picks up updates automatically when you're online.
  • Chrome on iPhone can also add it: tap Share in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen. Safari is the most reliable.
