How it works
  1. Set your week start and target hours (defaults to 40). Each week's punches are saved separately: pick a different Monday to see that week.
  2. Enter Clock In, Lunch Out and Lunch In as you punch them. Type the time as digits; the last two are the minutes (705 = 7:05, 105 = 1:05, 1130 = 11:30). Then pick AM or PM: a time counts only once its AM/PM is orange. On a phone, tap a time to open the keypad; tapping AM or PM saves it and moves to the next punch. On a keyboard, press A or P, or Tab to the AM/PM button and press Space to toggle.
  3. Leave today's Clock Out blank. "Clock out at" shows the exact time that brings the week to your target.
  4. If you have not taken lunch yet, that time assumes no further lunch. Add your lunch length to it if you still plan to take one.
  5. Hours show as h:mm and as decimal hours (8:15 · 8.25), the way Paycom shows them.
  6. Click or tap into a day to outline it. The first clear button then names that day (e.g. "Clear Monday") and empties only that day. Clear all erases everything. Both ask you to tap again to confirm.
